Fact-dependent real-estate planning

Evaluate potential deductions in the complete tax picture.

Real estate may create deduction and timing opportunities. Whether a strategy fits—and whether resulting losses are usable—depends on the property, activity, participation, passive-activity and passive-loss rules, at-risk limits, records, and the taxpayer’s facts.

Cost segregation and depreciation timing

Cost segregation can change the timing of depreciation deductions. An outside provider prepares the study; Golden Years reviews planning around the study, its timing, and the owner’s wider tax picture before projected deductions are treated as a benefit.

STR activity and material participation

Short-term rental classification and material participation are evaluated from the activity’s specific facts and supporting records. The analysis may affect how losses are treated under the passive-activity and at-risk rules.

Real Estate Professional Status

Qualifying as a real estate professional and materially participating in a rental activity are separate parts of the analysis. Loss usability still depends on the activity, passive-loss and at-risk rules, records, and the taxpayer’s complete facts.
